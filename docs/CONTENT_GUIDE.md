# Updating your website: a simple guide

Your website's content lives in the **Studio**, which is part of your website:
**https://your-domain/studio** (sign in with the email we invited, then bookmark it)

When you press **Publish**, the website updates by itself in about **3 minutes**. Refresh the page to see it.

---

## Write a new blog post

1. In the left menu click **Blog posts**, then the **pencil / +** button.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: English (required) and ಕನ್ನಡ (optional).
   - **Web address**: click **Generate**. Don't change it after publishing.
   - **Publish date**: today, or a future date to schedule the post.
   - **Summary**: one or two sentences. These show on the blog page and in Google.
   - **Cover image**: upload a photo and write what's in it ("Describe the image").
   - **Category** and **Author**.
   - **Article**: write in the English box. Kannada is optional: if you leave it empty, Kannada visitors see the English article.
3. Inside the article you can add:
   - **Heading / Sub-heading** from the style menu (use headings to break up long posts)
   - **Image**, **Highlighted tip**, **Shloka / mantra**, **YouTube video**
   - **Booking button**: add one near the end so readers can WhatsApp you
4. Optional but recommended:
   - **Questions & answers**: 2–3 common questions. These can show directly in Google.
   - **Links → Related services**: pick the service the post is about.
   - **SEO tab**: a custom Google title (under 60 characters) and description (under 160).
5. Press **Publish** (bottom right). Done!

> **Tip:** The subtitle under each post in the list shows **EN only** or **EN + ಕನ್ನಡ**, so you can see which posts still need translating.

## Change a post later
Open it, edit, and press **Publish** again. To take a post down, use the **⋯** menu → **Unpublish**.

## Tracking & ads (for your marketing team)

There is **no code to edit**: tags are added from the Studio, and they appear on **every page** automatically.

**Recommended: Google Tag Manager (one ID, set once)**
1. In [tagmanager.google.com](https://tagmanager.google.com) create a container for the website and copy its ID (looks like `GTM-ABC1234`).
2. In the Studio: **Site settings → Tracking & ads → Google Tag Manager ID**, paste the ID, press **Publish**.
3. From then on, add, change or remove any tag (Google Ads conversions, Analytics, Meta Pixel…) **inside Tag Manager** and press *Submit* there. The website does not need to change again.

**Without Tag Manager:** paste Google tag IDs instead in **Google tag IDs**, one per line: Analytics `G-XXXXXXX`, Google Ads `AW-123456789`.

> Paste **only the ID**, never the whole `<script>` snippet Google shows you. The Studio checks the format and shows a red message if it's wrong.

Changes are live about **3 minutes** after you publish. To check: open the website, then use Tag Manager's **Preview** button or Google's **Tag Assistant**.

## Good habits
- **Photos:** always fill in "Describe the image". Use real photos of Guruji and the centre; they build trust.
- **Honest wording:** please don't promise guaranteed results or "solved in 11 days". Google can penalise sites for this.
- **Same address everywhere:** the address in Site settings must match Google Maps exactly.
- **Post regularly:** one helpful article a month helps Google send you more visitors.

## What you can do yourself, and what needs a developer

**Yourself, any time, from the Studio:** all text, photos, blog posts, services, pujas, reviews, gallery, areas, FAQs, contact details, opening hours, SEO titles and descriptions, tracking and ads tags.

**Needs a developer:** a new *kind* of page or section, a change to the design or layout, a new website feature, or moving to a different server.

## Something went wrong?
- *I published but don't see the change.* Wait 5 minutes, then refresh. Still nothing? Message your developer.
- *I deleted something by mistake.* Open the document → **⋯** → **Review changes** to restore an earlier version.

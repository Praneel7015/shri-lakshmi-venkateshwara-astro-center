Mangal dosha before marriage: what it really means

By Preview editorial

30 August 2026 · 1 min read

Mangal dosha (also called Kuja dosha) is said to be present when Mars occupies the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house. By that definition, nearly half of all charts would have it, which is a sign that the rule is not meant to be applied mechanically.

When the dosha is cancelled

  • Mars in its own sign or exalted
  • Both partners having comparable Mangal dosha
  • Strong Jupiter aspecting Mars or the 7th house

Never cancel an alliance on the word "Mangal dosha" alone. Ask for the full chart comparison.
